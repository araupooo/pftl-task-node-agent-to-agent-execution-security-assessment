49: ### Registration
50: 
51: - Bond **100,000 PFT** on-chain (locked while active, returned in full on deregistration — not slashable)
52: - Link a **Task Node address** (on-chain address associated with the operator's Task Node identity)
53: - Complete a **test round** demonstrating proof-of-logits capability (successfully produce and submit valid logit commitments using the pinned model)
54: 
55: ### Liveness Requirements
56: 
57: - Oracle Nodes must participate in at least 1 of the last 4 scoring rounds
58: - Failure to meet liveness requirements results in the node being marked inactive
59: - Inactive nodes are excluded from quorum calculations
60: - Inactive nodes can reactivate by participating in the next scoring round
